Kaixing Zhang / 张凯星

Hi there! I am a Master's student in Computer Science at ShanghaiTech University, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Kan Ren.

My research focuses on LLM interpretability: understanding what large language models encode in their internal representations and how those representations give rise to their behavior.

Building on this understanding, I am particularly interested in leveraging representations to empower LLMs in agentic scenarios, from steering and enhancing their reasoning to guiding the self-evolution of agent skills.
